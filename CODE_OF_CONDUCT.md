# Code of Conduct

[Русская версия](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.ru.md)

## Our commitment

Contributors, maintainers, and users are expected to make participation in
this project respectful, professional, and free from harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and technically specific.
- Respect different experience levels and points of view.
- Focus criticism on code, configuration, evidence, and outcomes.
- Protect credentials, private correspondence, and customer information.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about project scope and operational safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, discrimination, or sexualized conduct.
- Publishing another person's private information or credentials.
- Deliberately unsafe instructions, spam, or malicious payloads.
- Repeated disruption, personal attacks, or refusal to follow moderation.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in the repository, its issue tracker, pull requests,
discussions, and public spaces where someone represents the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other
material that violates this policy and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact details at
[babaskin.dev](https://babaskin.dev/). Reports will be reviewed as
confidentially as practical.
